Will writing

Will writing service

When it comes to planning for the future and safeguarding your legacy, a professionally drafted Will is an essential document. In the United Kingdom, a notarised Will holds significant value, providing you and your loved ones with peace of mind, legal clarity and assurance that your wishes will be respected.

Creating a Will is a thoughtful and responsible act. It settles how your assets are distributed and how your affairs are managed after you are gone, and it takes that decision out of anyone else’s hands.

The process

From first enquiry to signed Will

Eight steps, the same every time, so you know what happens next at each stage.

  1. 1

    Initial Inquiry

    Contact us to express your interest in creating a Will.

  2. 2

    Completion of Questionnaire

    You will receive a questionnaire to fill out, where you are expected to provide honest and clear information about your priorities, desired outcomes and assets you wish to safeguard.

    Submit the completed questionnaire to us via email office@sikorskanotary.co.uk.

  3. 3

    Cost Confirmation

    Upon receiving your questionnaire, we assess your needs and confirm the cost of the Will writing service.

    There are two options available, set out below.

    A 50% deposit of the total cost is required at this stage.

  4. 4

    Comprehensive Case Assessment

    We will conduct a thorough assessment of your case based on the information you have provided.

    We will then proceed to draft your Will and email you a copy for your careful review and approval.

  5. 5

    Further Clarifications

    If you have additional questions or require further explanations, we can arrange an in-person meeting to discuss your preferences and requirements in detail.

  6. 6

    Drafting and Review of Wills

    Once you confirm that you are happy with everything, we will email you a final version of your Will for final review and approval.

  7. 7

    In-Person Will Signing

    We will schedule an in-person meeting with you and two witnesses for the formal signing of your Will to ensure that they are legally binding.

  8. 8

    Post-Service Support

    We will remain available to address any post-service questions or concerns you may have, ensuring your peace of mind.

Optional step

Registering the Will

We can assist in registering your Will with The National Will Register for an additional fee of £60 (inclusive of VAT and the National Will Register registration fee), or £120 (inclusive of VAT) to register a mirror pair of Wills.

The National Will Register exists to ensure no Will is left unknown or untraced at the time it is needed.
