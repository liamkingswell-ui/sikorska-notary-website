Fees

Fees & disbursements

The fees for notarial matters at Sikorska Notary are fixed to give you transparency and peace of mind.

Fees

Simple document

£90 minimum, plus VAT

We charge a minimum fee of £90 (plus VAT) for notarisation of a simple document such as passport, driving licence or one page letter.

Where a fixed fee is not possible

£240 per hour, plus VAT

If it is not possible to quote a fixed fee, we will provide you with the fee structure, applying an hourly rate of £240 (plus VAT), and further provide you with a proper estimate of the fee based on the information that you have provided at that time.

Any notarial services that are more complex will incur a higher fee. An individual fixed fee quote will be provided before we commence any notarial work.

Our fees are subject to VAT.

Payment can be made in pound sterling only by cash, card or bank transfer.

Disbursements

We will inform you in advance if any additional payments or disbursements are required in connection with your matter. You will be responsible for any disbursements incurred on your behalf, including:

  • Legalisation fees payable to the FCDO and/or Consulate/ Embassy/ Legalisation Agent
  • Translation or interpretation fees
  • Agent fees
  • Courier and postage costs
  • Companies House search fees
  • Verification fees for academic documents, certificates and corporate records
  • Other third-party fees

Changes to fees

Occasionally, unforeseen or unusual issues may arise during the course of your matter which could result in an increase to our fees or additional disbursements. For example, this may occur where further documents require notarisation, or where translation, legalisation or additional verification becomes necessary.

If you would like to obtain a full and comprehensive fee quote in your matter, please email office@sikorskanotary.co.uk.
